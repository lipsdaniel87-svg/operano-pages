Privacy Policy - Operano

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Purpose of Operano

Operano is a private desktop financial management application developed and used exclusively by its owner for personal and non-commercial purposes. Operano is not offered as a service to third parties.

2. Financial account information

Operano may connect to financial institutions through Enable Banking in order to retrieve financial account information relating solely to accounts owned or controlled by the user.

Depending on the information made available by the respective financial institution, this may include account identifiers, account details, balances and transaction information.

3. Purpose of processing

Financial information retrieved through Operano is used exclusively for the user's personal financial administration and management.

4. Storage and disclosure

Data retrieved by Operano is stored locally or within infrastructure privately controlled by the user.

Operano does not sell personal or financial information and does not provide such information to third parties for advertising or other commercial purposes.

5. Open Banking provider

Operano uses Enable Banking as a technical Open Banking connectivity provider. Information processed by Enable Banking is subject to the applicable privacy and data protection terms of Enable Banking.

6. Account authorization

Access to financial account information is initiated and authorized by the user through the respective financial institution. Such authorization may be revoked in accordance with the mechanisms provided by the financial institution or the Open Banking provider.

7. Website

This information page does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies or cookies for profiling purposes.
